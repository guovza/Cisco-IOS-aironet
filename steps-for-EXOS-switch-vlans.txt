AP is connected to switch port 3 and router uplink to port 1.


Initial EXOS switch (X430-24t) steps needed to get these two VLANs working:


Step 1 — Create VLAN 20


create vlan "IOT" tag 20


Step 2 — Add VLANs to router uplink (Port 1)

Port 1 must be a trunk:

configure vlan IOT add ports 1 tagged
configure vlan Default add ports 1 untagged


(Default = VLAN 10)


Step 3 — Configure AP uplink (Port 3)

Port 3:
    •   VLAN 10 = untagged (native)
    •   VLAN 20 = tagged
    

configure vlan IOT add ports 3 tagged
configure vlan Default add ports 3 untagged
